Our story

Born in Plettenberg Bay

John's officially launched in 2027 as a proudly local fashion brand created in Plettenberg Bay by Mandiluve Mganu in collaboration with other clothing business enterprises. The brand was founded on the belief that fashion should feel personal, refined, and empowering.

From modest beginnings to a sought-after boutique identity, John's emerged as a celebration of elegance, craftsmanship, and community. Every collection reflects a fusion of premium style and culturally rooted artistry.

Mandiluve Mganu Founder & Creative Director
